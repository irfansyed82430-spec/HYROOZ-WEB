Support

Need help? We're here.

Everything you need to know about our garment cuts, custom printing engine, and shipping times.

Which garment cuts do you offer?

Regular fit tees, heavyweight oversized tees, boxy pullover hoodies, minimalist sweatshirts, and drop-shoulder boxy sweatshirts, each in curated colourways.

What file formats can I upload?

High-resolution SVG, PNG, and TIFF files work best. You can also use personal photography or build typographic layouts in our built-in font studio.

How long does printing and delivery take?

Orders are dispatched within 48 hours across India in weather-sealed packaging, with SMS updates at each step.

What is the free audit for ₹1?

For ₹1 we review your design files for print readiness, colour, resolution, and placement before you order.

Which payment methods do you accept?

UPI, NetBanking, credit cards, and Cash on Delivery, with secure payment processing.

How do I track my order?

Use the Order Tracking link in the footer. You will also receive live dispatch updates by SMS.
